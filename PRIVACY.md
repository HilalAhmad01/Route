# Route — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Route is a Delhi NCR metro route planner by Hilal Ahmad (package `app.metroline`).

**Short version: Route does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.** It has no
internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone even if it wanted to.

## What Route collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
third-party SDKs. Route does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or
advertising ID, and it requests no Android permissions at all.

## What stays on your phone

Route keeps a few things in its private app storage so the app remembers them next time:

- Your bookmarks: the label you type (for example "Home") and the station it points to
- Your recent and saved trips (pairs of stations)
- Your smart card / token fare preference
- Launcher shortcuts for your bookmarks (the "From Home" entry when you hold the app icon),
  which Android's launcher keeps on your device

This data is never sent to me or anyone else. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer. You can remove it by deleting bookmarks and recent trips inside the
app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling Route.

## When you choose to share or leave the app

- **Share a route.** Route draws a picture of the route, saves it temporarily in its cache and
  hands it, with the directions as text, to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. From that
  point the app you chose handles it under its own privacy policy.
- **Book ticket on WhatsApp.** This opens a `wa.me` link to DMRC's official WhatsApp ticketing
  number with the message "Hi" typed in. WhatsApp and DMRC handle everything after that. Route
  sends no trip details, does not see the conversation and stores nothing from it.
- **Installing from Google Play.** Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to the install.
  Route receives nothing from Google Play about you.

## Children

Route is a general-purpose utility, not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it
collects none from children either.

## Third-party material

Station and line data come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Details are in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Route is independent and is not affiliated
with DMRC, NMRC, NCRTC or Rapid Metro Gurugram.
